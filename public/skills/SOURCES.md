# Skill logo sources

Downloaded SVG assets served locally by the portfolio.

- Logos other than Gemini, OpenAI, Cursor, and Claude: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), from the `icons/<technology>/<technology>-original.svg` files. AWS uses `amazonwebservices-original-wordmark.svg`. Devicon's MIT license is included in `DEVICON-LICENSE.txt`.
- Gemini: [Simple Icons Google Gemini SVG](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/googlegemini.svg), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
- Figma, Railway, and Vercel: Devicon original SVGs, under the included MIT license.
- PostgreSQL and GitHub Actions: [PostgreSQL original SVG](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/postgresql/postgresql-original.svg) and [GitHub Actions original SVG](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/icons/githubactions/githubactions-original.svg), under the included Devicon MIT license.
- OpenAI (shown as Codex): [Simple Icons v15 SVG](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/15.0.0/icons/openai.svg), under CC0. Codex uses the OpenAI brand mark.
- Cursor and Claude: [Simple Icons v16](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0/icons), under CC0.

Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners.
